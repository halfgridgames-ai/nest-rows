How to roost

  • ●Every nest tree wants exactly one owl, landed on one of the branches growing out of it. Squares with no branch are open sky.
  • ●An owl guards one nest only, and owls never sit next to each other, not even diagonally.
  • ●The numbers say how many owls roost in each row and column.
  • ●Tap a branch once to land an owl, twice to leave a grass tuft (a note that no owl goes there), and a third time to clear it. Tufts are optional, but on big boards they make the owls easier to find.
  • ●An owl that breaks a rule gets a coral ring the moment it lands, with the rule explained. Hints settle the next branch for you.
